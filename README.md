### 💳 Fintech Product Performance & User Experience Analysis

**NorthStar Fintech UK Ltd. | Product Analytics Project**

Analyzed **1,500 product-interaction records** covering January 2025 to June 2026 to evaluate fintech feature performance, user experience, transaction outcomes, customer satisfaction, retention, and revenue.

**Key areas analyzed:**

* Cleaned and validated data containing missing values, duplicates, inconsistent categories, mixed date formats, invalid ratings, and dirty GBP fields.
* Evaluated **feature adoption, task-completion rate, transaction-success rate, error rate, and customer retention**.
* Identified user confusion, journey drop-offs, task-time issues, and support-related problems affecting the user experience.
* Compared product performance across **customer groups, platforms, and acquisition channels**.
* Evaluated **A/B test experiments** to assess whether product changes and Variant B performed better than the control.
* Analyzed customer satisfaction, NPS, support experiences, and product revenue to identify areas requiring improvement.
* Developed data-driven recommendations to improve product usability, reduce errors and friction, strengthen retention, and protect revenue.

**Tools:** Excel | Power Query | Pivot Tables | PivotCharts | Slicers | Data Visualization

**Outcome:** Identified underperforming and high-performing features, major UX pain points, customer segments experiencing greater difficulty, and opportunities for product improvements based on performance and experimental evidence.
